Small and honest beats big and convincing, three times over.

─────────────────
01 · She skipped asking because she already knew. She was wrong.
Jason Cohen × Paul Graham
The expert is not less accurate than the novice. The expert simply stops checking, and stops earlier. If you can feel the answer before the question is out, that feeling is the reason to ask.
→ https://longform.asmartbear.com/expert-harmful/

─────────────────
02 · A hand-picked team proves the model works. It also proves what you removed.
Marty Cagan × John Cutler
Everyone treats the pilot's number as the finding. The number was never in doubt; you staffed it with your best people and cleared their path. The finding is the path-clearing itself, written down and priced.
→ https://www.svpg.com/the-politics-of-pilot-teams/

─────────────────
03 · Training everyone at once fails the way a thin network fails.
Teresa Torres × Andrew Chen
Company-wide training is bought because it is legible on a slide: everyone covered, one line item. Two people who agreed to meet on Thursday will outlast it, for the same reason a dense corner of a network outlasts a broad launch.
→ https://www.producttalk.org/learning-together-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/
