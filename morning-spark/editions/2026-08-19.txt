01 · Hand the buyer the ruler, not the score
Nikki Marinsek × April Dunford
Take your strongest performance claim and ask what a customer would need in order to reproduce it themselves next Tuesday. Build that before you say the number again.

02 · The build got cheap, the slot did not
Andrew Chen × Ben Thompson
Open your own home screen and count how many of those apps you would swap for a better version this week. That count, not the market report, is the size of the opening you are building into.
→ https://andrewchen.substack.com/p/ai-will-change-how-we-build-startups

─────────────────
03 · A request with no measure is an order in disguise
Marty Cagan × Gibson Biddle
Take the last thing you asked someone to build and write the single number that would move if it worked. If nothing comes to mind, you wrote a spec, and the person building it owns none of the outcome.
→ https://www.svpg.com/stakeholders-and-the-product-model/
