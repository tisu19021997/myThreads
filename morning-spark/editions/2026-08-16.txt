What you can defend, what you can price, what you can say early.

─────────────────
01 · The hater's one-liner is a spec
Andrew Chen × Brian Balfour
Chen says every category has a skeptic's one-liner, and the real danger is quietly accepting it. Balfour says the model, meaning price, retention and channel, decides what a product can become. Put together: the anti-pitch is free research into the model that killed the last ten attempts.
→ https://andrewchen.substack.com/p/the-anti-pitch-one-liner-rebuttals

─────────────────
02 · Five stories become a decision once they cost money
Melissa Perri × Teresa Torres
Perri argues that scoring frameworks are what a team reaches for when leadership has not set a strategy, and that real prioritization runs on dollars. Torres argues that evidence only reads clearly under one target outcome. Put together: discovery earns its keep the day you price the delay.
→ https://melissaperri.com/blog/2019/10/31/prioritization

─────────────────
03 · Empowerment ends where your clarity stops
Petra Wille × Marty Cagan
Wille says leadership is directional clarity practised at a longer and longer horizon, deliberately getting less concrete as it stretches. Cagan says empowered teams get problems rather than features. Put together: how far your team can run without you is not a matter of trust, it is the length of your horizon.
→ https://www.producttalk.org/stepping-into-leadership-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/
