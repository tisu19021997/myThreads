01 · Automating yourself starts by admitting what you do
Grace Clarke × John Cutler
Grace Clarke rebuilt her business on tools she wrote herself, and the load-bearing file is a voice guide. Cutler's warning is that automating a capability costs you the reps. Both are true, and the reconciliation is that you can only automate the part you can articulate.

02 · The best moat is arithmetic, not strategy
Jason Cohen × Ben Thompson
Cohen's materiality threshold says a big company must reject a one million dollar niche. Aggregation theory says the biggest companies are the ones with the least room to serve anyone unusual. Together they describe a protected space you get for free.
→ https://longform.asmartbear.com/startup-beats-incumbent/

03 · Make something people want, and you have thirty days to find out
Andrew Chen × Paul Graham
Chen has watched thousands of retention curves and reports that they never bend upward from a bad start. Read next to make something people want, that is not a note about metrics, it is a schedule: the answer arrives early and iteration cannot appeal it.
