Where to go

  • Hshop40/12 Lu Gia, P.15, Q.11, HCMCMaker favourite. Modules, sensors, dev boards. Ships nationwide in 1-3 days.
  • Nshop1 Bui Xuan Phai, Tay Thanh, Tan Phu, HCMCSame category as Hshop. Cross-check stock and price between the two.
  • TheGioiICQ.10, HCMCSerious component distributor. Go here for discretes, passives, connectors, ICs by part number.
  • ICDayRoiHCMCGood for odd single components and ICs.
  • NhatTaoCho Nhat Tao, Q.10, HCMCThe physical market. Go in person for tools, wire, connectors, salvage, and to handle parts before buying. Haggle.
  • ShopeeonlineCheapest for generic tools, craft supplies, consumables. Check seller rating; 2-5 days.
  • JLCPCBonline, ships to VNPCB fab. 5 boards from about 2 USD plus shipping. DHL to VN is roughly 5-10 days.
  • SQFMIonline, ships from abroadWatchy itself. Longest lead time of anything on this list. Order it early or plan a substitute.
  • AliExpressonline, ships from abroadFor parts no HCMC shop stocks. Cheap, but two to five weeks to Vietnam. Order in week 1 or not at all.
