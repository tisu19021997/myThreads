Morning Spark · Monday, July 27 · Ed. 01
Three collisions on speed, agency, and the discipline of subtraction.

─────────────────
01 · Your velocity is not a metric
Marty Cagan × Gibson Biddle
Cagan's paradox is that AI adoption is climbing while results sit still, and the strong product companies are pulling further ahead rather than closing the gap. Biddle's answer predates AI: name the leading indicator you can move weekly, and let it judge your speed.
→ https://www.svpg.com/the-ai-productivity-paradox/

─────────────────
02 · The bottleneck moved from hands to wants
Ben Thompson × Paul Graham
Thompson's line is that AI still needs agency, it just does not need agency from that many people. That makes taste and initiative the constraint, not headcount or capital.
→ https://stratechery.com/2026/agents-over-bubbles/

─────────────────
03 · Your calendar is your positioning statement
Jason Cohen × April Dunford
Cohen catalogues what stopping creates space for: better customers, one real metric, a few modes of differentiation. Dunford names the thing that space is for, which is one clear alternative you beat.
→ https://longform.asmartbear.com/focus/
