01 · Five jobs became one. The skill survived; the title did not.
Christian Crumlish × Harry Braverman
Five jobs made a book at Sybex in 1992, and the PC merged three of them into one. The typesetters who walked were not protecting a skill, they were protecting a title. Braverman's century pushed conception up and execution down; this is that split running backwards, with the planning landing on whoever is nearest.

02 · Imitation learning stalled, so hand-written robot code came back.
Ken Goldberg × Richard Sutton
Berkeley and NVIDIA announced agentic robotics this week: no demonstration data, coding agents writing readable model-based code, 99 percent success on tasks where imitation learning got 20. The bitter lesson said human knowledge plateaus. The part that plateaued was the human doing the entering.

03 · 98.7 percent never reach the expensive model.
Jina Yoon × Abraham Wald
PostHog runs two narrow yes-or-no checks before any frontier call, and 98.7 percent of session summaries never reach the expensive model. The threshold is 0.4 where a missed name is the costly error and 0.85 where a false alarm is. Wald put the stopping rule inside the test in 1945; this puts the question inside it.
