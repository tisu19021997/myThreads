01 · Google should build a shrine to a fruit fly
Matt Webb × Gibson Biddle
Webb wants a fruit fly's connectome engraved on silicon and put somewhere you can light a candle. Biddle's hard-to-copy axis is expensive for the same structural reason. Free to perform, free to copy: either way nothing was actually said.
→ https://interconnected.org/home/2026/09/26/connectome

─────────────────
02 · Runway does not buy survival, it buys frames
Cedric Chin × Jason Cohen
Chin's survivors bought time with a balance sheet and spent it hunting for a frame. Cohen's bootstrapper has no time to buy and so has to choose early. RIM had the money and never chose. Cash does not remove the decision, it only sets its deadline.
→ https://commoncog.com/how-to-handle-disruptive-change/

─────────────────
03 · Retention counts who never left. Ask who came back.
Meg Adams × Lenny Rachitsky
Adams reads her culture off the engineers who left, looked at the alternative, and asked to come back. Rachitsky reads a product off the curve that flattens. Both are ignoring the people who simply have not moved. And the honest version of the metric admits its own hole: the ones who never wrote back are not in the sample.
→ https://leaddev.com/culture/build-an-engineering-team-people-want-to-come-back-to
