01 · An exact invoice is not a model of anything
John Cutler × Jason Cohen
AI spend is measured to the cent and understood not at all. Before the next invoice, write one sentence saying what the spend is betting on, and name the number that would move first if the bet were right.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-437-tokens-hours-points-and-other

─────────────────
02 · Speed is worth something only where it deletes the step that killed the idea
Yana Welinder × Marty Cagan
Go back to the ideas you killed on cost rather than on merit. One of them has a middle step a model can now do, and that one is the only place your new speed is worth anything.
→ https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-how-a-solo-founder-used

─────────────────
03 · A role defined by what gets built is a role about the backlog
Brian Balfour × Melissa Perri
Wanting growth is free. Doing the unglamorous half is not. Look at last week's calendar and count the hours that went to feeding a backlog against the hours that went to deciding what the business should be worth.
→ https://brianbalfour.com/essays/growth-is-optional
