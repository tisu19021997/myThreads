01 · Let the market price your roadmap
Ben Thompson × Melissa Perri
Output metrics are graded by the same people who picked the work. A rental price is not. Thompson's move is to install one number nobody in the building controls, then let it quietly kill whatever cannot clear it.

02 · Your navigators are why it stays broken
John Cutler × Andrew Chen
Cutler's tell is that insiders move around happily. That is not evidence of coherence, it is evidence that navigation became a job. Every new dependency doc is one more reason to keep the maze standing.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-434-how-maps-can-hide-problems

03 · Growth proves love. It does not buy time.
Paul Graham × Gibson Biddle
Compounding is arithmetic. The number of months is strategy. Graham is right that you cannot cheat your way to a bigger market, and Biddle is right that you can be copied out of one. Only one of the two numbers is under attack.
→ https://paulgraham.com/earn.html
