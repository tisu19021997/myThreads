Morning Spark · Wednesday, August 5 · Ed. 10
What stays scarce when the building gets cheap.

─────────────────
01 · AI does not spring the build trap. It pays the rent.
John Cutler × Melissa Perri
Cutler asks which direction AI is accelerating, not whether it is fast. Perri's build trap is the direction most teams are already pointed. Cheap enough to keep is how a dead bet survives the meeting where it should have died.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-430-incubate-compound-refinance

─────────────────
02 · The model was the commodity. The years were not.
Teresa Torres × Ben Thompson
Snapbar did not beat AI-native startups by being more AI. It stacked commodity models on fourteen years nobody else had. When the model layer commoditizes, Thompson's rule says the margin moves next door, and next door is domain knowledge you cannot buy this quarter.
→ https://www.producttalk.org/from-covid-pivot-to-ai-world-building-how-snapbar-reinvented-the-photo-experience/

─────────────────
03 · A career is a loop, not a funnel.
Molly Graham × Brian Balfour
Graham says the pattern only looks designed in hindsight. Balfour says you never design the output either, only the loop. Same shape. A plan is a forecast you will miss; a rule is a step you can run tomorrow morning.
→ https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-if-youre-not-supposed-to-have
