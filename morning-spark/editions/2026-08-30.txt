Three claims that cannot fail. None of them is evidence.

─────────────────
01 · The backlash is everywhere. Check whether anything moved.
Andrew Sharp × Teresa Torres
Take the loudest request you have heard this month and go looking for the behaviour behind it. If nobody ever worked around its absence, you are holding an opinion, not demand.
→ https://stratechery.com/2026/internet-hype-and-real-world-change/

─────────────────
02 · You cannot judge the skill. You can judge the hour.
Jason Cohen × Mark Zuckerberg
After the next interview, write down the one thing you did not know before it started. If the page is blank, you were not evaluating them. You were being pitched.
→ https://longform.asmartbear.com/hire-better-than-you/

─────────────────
03 · If nobody in the room would write a different sentence, it is not strategy.
John Cutler × Roger Martin
Write the biggest bet you are running as one sentence somebody could disagree with, then ask two people to rate their confidence in it. If both say very confident, you have not made a choice yet.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-437-transition-week-and-some
