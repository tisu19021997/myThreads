Three things nobody can delegate: context, voice, and the argument.

─────────────────
01 · The agent works off-screen. Your research was watching the screen.
Ben Thompson × Teresa Torres
Every discovery habit you have assumes a human stays in the loop long enough to be watched. Agents remove the loop, not the user. Decide now what your product records when nobody is looking at it.
→ https://stratechery.com/2026/the-iphones-last-stand/

─────────────────
02 · We are delighted to announce that nobody believes this sentence.
Andrew Chen × Lenny Rachitsky
Read your last product announcement out loud. If you would not say it in a bar, it was written for the approval chain, not the reader. Cut the approval chain before you hire another writer.
→ https://andrewchen.substack.com/p/corpospeak-why-you-still-sound-like

─────────────────
03 · One metric that matters, and nobody can argue with it.
Brian Balfour × April Dunford
Pick the metric that makes part of your roadmap obviously wrong. If every project still qualifies, you have written a slogan and called it focus.
→ https://brianbalfour.com/essays/north-star-metric-growth
