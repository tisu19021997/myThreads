Morning Spark · Monday, September 7 · Ed. 43
Define good yourself. Stop guarding the gate. Price your hour.

─────────────────
01 · A benchmark tells you where everyone landed. Not whether you are right.
Teresa Torres × Lenny Rachitsky
Open the dashboard you check every morning. For each number, say out loud who decided what counts as good. If the answer is a vendor, a template or a blog post, that number is orientation, not evidence.
→ https://www.producttalk.org/ai-evals/

─────────────────
02 · Code review was never one job. AI just removed the slack that hid it.
Rachel Laycock × Marty Cagan
List what your pull request process actually delivers: quality, security, mentoring, alignment. For each one, name the person accountable if it stops happening tomorrow. The items with no name are the reason the queue exists.
→ https://martinfowler.com/rachels-ramblings/code-review.html

─────────────────
03 · Ten seconds to write. Ten minutes to read. Someone is paying that.
Sean Goedecke × Jason Cohen
Think of the last long message you had to read twice to find the point. Guess what your hour is worth, then what theirs cost. If the ratio is a hundred to one, replying with let us take five minutes on a call is not rude. It is a correction.
→ https://seangoedecke.com/how-to-protect-yourself-from-workslop/
