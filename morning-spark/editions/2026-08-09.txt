Before you copy it, ask who it was actually built for.

─────────────────
01 · Your strategy deck has a target customer
John Cutler × April Dunford
Dunford tests positioning against the alternatives a real buyer is weighing. A strategy pyramid never faces that test, because its audience is paid to stay in the room. Read today's top slide to someone who can walk out.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-435-20-unfiltered-operating-takes

─────────────────
02 · The tactic travels, the constraint stays home
Jason Cohen × Brian Balfour
Cohen says there is no map, because trends cannot predict outliers. Balfour narrows it: the tactic stood on an ARPU, a channel, and a way of charging. Before you copy it, write those three numbers down for the company you took it from, then for yours.
→ https://longform.asmartbear.com/compass/

─────────────────
03 · Your worst prompt is a prototype
Teresa Torres × Marty Cagan
Torres runs one to-do item through AI every day and lets it fail. Cagan named that motion years ago: build to learn, where the only deliverable is an answer. Take one task off today's list, spend fifteen minutes, and keep the finding rather than the output.
→ https://www.producttalk.org/ai-shaped-problems-all-things-product-podcast-with-teresa-torres-petra-wille/
