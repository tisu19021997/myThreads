NothingCareers: Hardware Interaction Designerrole description / 10 min
  • Underline every noun that is a skill: buttons, screens, sound, voice, apps, light, motion, haptics.
  • Underline every verb that is a behaviour: conceive, prototype, communicate, iterate.
  • Note which ones you can already do today as a software engineer. It will be more than you expect, and fewer than you need.
02BENCH30 min

Hand-write the rubric on one card, eight lines, one per requirement. This card lives on your desk for 168 days. Then pick the first of your three autopsy objects, something with exactly one control, and write down every single thing it does.

  • The eight lines: multimodal interaction, hardware and software integration, physical prototyping, interaction reasoning, technical implementation, product concept development, attention to detail, explaining both why and how.
  • Object one: a lighter, a kettle, a doorbell, a lamp switch, a mechanical watch crown. One control only.
  • For every interaction write three columns: what you do, what it does, how you know it did it.
