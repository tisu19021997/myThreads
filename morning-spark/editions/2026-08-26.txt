01 · You automated the review, not the judgment
Ryan Carson × Teresa Torres
Carson automated the expensive half of shipping and kept the cheap half on paper. The paper is the interesting part. It is where the decisions live, and it did not get bigger when the agents did.

02 · Nobody moved the bottleneck, they moved the story
John Cutler × Brian Balfour
Cutler's sharpest line is that the bottleneck has shifted to conversations about how the bottleneck has shifted. Test it on your own week: how much of it went to arguing about where the work is stuck, versus being stuck in it.
→ https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-427-the-bottleneck-strike-again

─────────────────
03 · A promotion is the most copyable thing about you
Cliff Maxwell × Gibson Biddle
Maxwell's framing is blunt about timing: work may feel unstable now, and this is likely the most stable it will feel for a while. A ladder is a bet that the rungs above you will still be there. Progress is a bet on yourself instead.
→ https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-figure-out-your-next-career
