01 · The policy lands quietly because the anger already left the room
Charity Majors × Teresa Torres

02 · The bottle's neck is not the bottleneck, yours is
Jim Nielsen × Melissa Perri
Nielsen is arguing that some constraints are design, not failure, and the test is whether removing one makes a mess. Perri names the mess: more output arriving at a team whose capacity to decide, review and learn did not move an inch.
→ https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2026/bottlenecks/

─────────────────
03 · Ten million dollars for the outputs, and the experience was not in the box
Ben Lorica × Jason Cohen
Lorica's warning is that companies fret about their archive leaking when the real loss is that the valuable part was never captured: a decision severed from its outcome, an exception that died in a chat thread. Cohen's filter finishes the thought, since a bankruptcy court can sell an archive, and nobody can sell you the habit of recording why the fix worked.
→ https://gradientflow.substack.com/p/what-counts-as-valuable-company-data
